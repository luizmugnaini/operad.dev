About

Hello there! I’m Luiz Mugnaini, a Brazilian mathematician and a Master’s student in Computer Science at the University of São Paulo (USP).

My interests mostly span through art, philosophy, and mathematics. I’m invested in the studying volumetric representation of art through the process of sculpting. I’m still in a very exploratory state of my studies in philosophy, primarily in epistemology. Within mathematics, I’m currently studying higher structures, mainly in the context of geometry.

I love to implement software from scratch, mostly in C and C++. I also sometimes do some scripting in Python and Lua. With the advent of AI, however, the craft of programming has mostly lost the value it had for me, thus I now rarely spend any time programming — which is unfortunate.

I previously did research in:

  • Discrete differential geometry and its applications to computer graphics, together with my advisor Mauricio Collares and co-advisor Sinai Robins.
  • Abstract homotopy theory via model categories under the supervision of Ivan Struchiner.

Feel free to explore my website and connect with me. I’m always eager to exchange some cool ideas.

I’m currently available for contract work on a part-time basis.

Note: I use em-dashes. I setup my blog so that triple dashes are converted to an em-dash. I’ll not allow a human construct to be appropriated by artificial stupidity.
