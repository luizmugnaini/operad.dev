Hello there! I'm Luiz Mugnaini, a Brazilian mathematician and a Master's student in Computer Science at
the University of São Paulo (USP).

My interests mostly span through art, philosophy, and mathematics. I'm invested in the studying
volumetric representation of art through the process of sculpting. I'm still in a very exploratory
state of my studies in philosophy, primarily in epistemology. Within mathematics, I'm currently
studying higher structures, mainly in the context of geometry.

I love to implement software from scratch, mostly in C and C++. I also sometimes do some scripting
in Python and Lua. With the advent of AI, however, the craft of programming has mostly lost the
value it had for me, thus I now rarely spend any time programming --- which is unfortunate.

I previously did research in:
- [_Discrete differential geometry_](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~kmcrane/Projects/DDG/) and its
  applications to computer graphics, together with my advisor [Mauricio
  Collares](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=NzpLYCEAAAAJ) and co-advisor [Sinai
  Robins](https://sites.google.com/site/sinairobins).
- [_Abstract homotopy theory_](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/homotopy+theory) via [_model
  categories_](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/model+category) under the supervision of [Ivan
  Struchiner](https://www.ime.usp.br/~ivanstru).

Feel free to explore my website and connect with me. I'm always eager to exchange some cool ideas.
